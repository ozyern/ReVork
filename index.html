About ReVork

ReVork is a community project that ports OxygenOS 16 and ColorOS 16 to OnePlus flagships that no longer receive official updates. Each port is built for one specific phone, so the camera, modem, fingerprint reader and display tuning keep working the way they did on stock firmware, while you get the newer interface, features and security fixes of the latest OnePlus and OPPO software.

Every device has its own page with the full list of builds, newest first. The OnePlus 9 Pro ROM page and the OnePlus 9 ROM page also carry realmeUI 7 builds. The OnePlus 9R ROM page, the OnePlus 8 Pro ROM page and the OnePlus 8T ROM page list OxygenOS 16 and ColorOS 16.

Before you flash a build you will usually need a custom recovery and, for some devices, a super_empty.img payload. Both are collected in the recovery and super_empty download terminal, where you select your device, the component you need and the variant that matches your phone.

ROM builds are hosted on SourceForge and recovery images come from the project's GitHub releases. Always back up your data and check that the model number on your phone matches the hardware you select before you flash anything. Questions, bug reports and release announcements live in the ReVork Telegram group.
